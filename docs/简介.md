## 一句话简介 (Summary)

### English

Create addon where steam is the power source: burn fuel for heat, boil water, then run engines, flywheels and steam-driven machines off what you produce.

## 详情 (Description)

### English

#### Create: Letter's in Steam

Steam in most Create addons is a gadget bolted onto existing kinetics. Here it is the other way round: fuel only exists to make heat, heat only exists to make steam, and everything that spins or processes is driven by that steam. The loop is built so that each step can be measured, tuned and optimised instead of being one opaque block.

**fuel → heat → steam → rotation and processing**

#### Making steam

- **Bronze Boiler**: holds 10,000 mB of water and turns it into steam at 12 mB/t at full efficiency. Pipe water in, take steam out of the top.
- **Bronze Solid Burner**: burns coal-like fuels. Heat ramps up over roughly 60 seconds and decays over roughly 30.
- **Bronze Fluid Burner**: the same machine for liquid fuels, injected from any side with pipes, holding 1,000 mB.
- **Bronze Solar Boiler**: steam without fuel. It scans up to 16 blocks above itself, treats glass, leaves and slabs as transparent, and needs real daylight (configurable). Clear weather gives 100%, rain 50%, thunder 25%, and light level 8 or higher from any light source gives 50% - so a well-lit indoor setup still works, just slower. Dimensions without a skylight count as fully sunlit.

Heat is a 0-100 scale, and the boiler converts it into production in four tiers: nothing below 25, then 25%, 50%, 75%, and a full 100% at maximum heat. A combustion chamber has to sit directly underneath a boiler, or the boiler receives no heat at all.

#### Superheated steam

Fuels marked as superheated (a block tag, so any mod's fuel can opt in) raise a burner's heat ceiling to 200. Once a boiler passes 100 heat it produces **superheated steam** instead of steam, and its production climbs from 75% at 125 heat towards full output at the ceiling. A config switch decides whether that superheated output displaces the regular steam the boiler was storing.

Superheated steam is not just a rename: an engine warms up twice as fast on it, burns half as much of it, and delivers 1.5 times the output. All three multipliers are config values.

#### Turning steam into stress

- **Bronze Steam Flywheel**: the output block of the set. Wrench it to choose which side the rotation leaves from.
- **Bronze Steam Engine**: sits against a boiler, draws steam from its back, and drives the flywheel in front of it. By default it runs at 32 RPM with a 16384 SU stress capacity and consumes 12 mB/t from a 32,000 mB internal buffer. Drawing straight out of a boiler only transfers 75% of the steam, so layout matters. It takes 2 seconds to warm up and keeps running for 20 ticks after the steam stops.
- **Bronze Steam Pump**: bridges a boiler and an engine that cannot sit next to each other. It needs no stress and no rotation, but it can only move steam and superheated steam - nothing else.
- **Sealed Steam Container**: a portable 64,000 mB tank. Right-click a boiler, a fluid tank or a steam engine to fill it, sneak-right-click to keep drawing, right-click again to feed the steam back.

One boiler at full efficiency feeds exactly one engine at 12 mB/t, so scaling up means building more complete boiler groups rather than making a bigger boiler.

#### Steam-powered processing

These machines need both rotation and a constant steam feed, and they shut down the moment the steam runs out:

- **Steam Hammer**: a powered press, up to 64 recipes in parallel.
- **Steam Mixer**: a powered mixer that must be mounted directly above a basin, 16 recipes in parallel.
- **Steam Saw**: a powered saw, 16 recipes in parallel, working four times faster than a mechanical saw. It can also be mounted on contraptions.
- **Advanced Spout**: fills items underneath in 5 ticks, with 1 to 20 parallel operations adjustable through a wrench-opened side panel. It cannot be filled by hand - fluids must be piped in.
- **Bronze Basin**: accepts several fluids at once, so mixing and compacting recipes that need more than one fluid become possible, and every fluid slot holds 16 times a regular basin's amount.
- **Bronze Depot**: an item buffer built on Create's depot behaviour, sized for the rest of the set.

#### Compatibility and extension

- **KubeJS**: register custom boilers, solar boilers, burners and engine/flywheel pairs with capacity, production, max heat, material and model parameters. Fuel recipes come from `solid_fuel(item, time).superheated()` and `fluid_fuel(fluid, time)`.
- **JEI**: solid and fluid fuel categories, plus heated and superheated information on recipes.
- **Ponder**: built-in scenes cover the boiler group, the solar boiler, the pump, the spout and the three processing machines.
- **Create**: every machine joins the normal stress network, and the saw works on contraptions.
- Every number above is a common config value - heat tiers, efficiency steps, the three superheated multipliers, parallel counts and capacities can all be changed.

#### Installation

Minecraft 1.20.1, Forge 47.x, Create 6.0 or newer. Drop the jar into `mods`; Create is the only required dependency.

### 中文

在多数 Create 附属里, 蒸汽只是挂在原有力学系统上的一个小玩意. 本模组反过来做: 燃料只为生热, 热量只为产汽, 凡是会转的或会加工的东西都由蒸汽驱动. 整条链条的每一步都可测量, 可调参, 可优化, 而不是一个不透明的方块.

**燃料 → 热量 → 蒸汽 → 旋转动力与加工**

#### 产汽

- **青铜锅炉**: 存水 10000 mB, 满效率时以 12 mB/t 产汽. 管道进水, 顶部出汽.
- **青铜固体燃烧室**: 烧煤类燃料, 约 60 秒升到满热, 停机后约 30 秒散热.
- **青铜流体燃烧室**: 同一台机器烧液体燃料, 任意侧面用管道注入, 容量 1000 mB.
- **青铜太阳能锅炉**: 不用燃料的产汽方式. 向上检查 16 格, 玻璃, 树叶, 台阶视为透明, 且需要真正的日光(可配置). 晴天 100%, 下雨 50%, 雷雨 25%, 任意光源达到光照 8 时以 50% 工作 - 所以照亮的室内也能跑, 只是慢. 无天空光的维度按全日照处理.

热量是 0 到 100 的标尺, 锅炉按四档把它换算成产率: 25 以下不产汽, 之后依次 25%, 50%, 75%, 满热 100%. 燃烧室必须紧贴锅炉正下方, 否则锅炉得不到任何热量.

#### 过热蒸汽

被标记为过热燃料的燃料(方块标签, 任何模组的燃料都能加入)会把燃烧室的热量上限提到 200. 锅炉热量超过 100 后生产**过热蒸汽**而不是普通蒸汽, 产率从 125 热量时的 75% 一路升到上限的满产. 有一项配置决定过热产物是否取代锅炉原本储存的普通蒸汽.

过热蒸汽不只是换个名字: 引擎用它时预热速度翻倍, 消耗量减半, 输出变为 1.5 倍. 这三个倍率都是配置项.

#### 把蒸汽变成应力

- **青铜蒸汽飞轮**: 这一套的动力输出端. 用扳手选择动力从哪一侧取出.
- **青铜蒸汽引擎**: 贴着锅炉放置, 从背面抽汽, 驱动它前方的飞轮. 默认 32 RPM, 应力容量 16384 SU, 从 32000 mB 的内部缓冲中以 12 mB/t 消耗. 直接从锅炉抽汽只能转移 75% 的蒸汽, 所以摆放方式很关键. 预热 2 秒, 蒸汽中断后还能维持 20 tick 才停机.
- **青铜蒸汽泵**: 用来连接无法相邻放置的锅炉与引擎. 它不需要应力也不需要旋转, 但只能输送蒸汽与过热蒸汽, 别的流体一概不行.
- **密封蒸汽容器**: 64000 mB 的便携汽罐. 右击锅炉, 储罐或蒸汽引擎注汽, 潜行右击持续抽取, 再次右击把蒸汽放回去.

一台满效率的锅炉正好喂饱一台 12 mB/t 的引擎, 所以扩大产能意味着多建完整的锅炉机组, 而不是把锅炉做大.

#### 蒸汽加工

这些机器同时需要旋转动力与持续的蒸汽供应, 蒸汽一断就立刻停机:

- **蒸汽锻锤**: 动力压机, 最高并行 64 份配方.
- **蒸汽搅拌机**: 动力搅拌机, 必须架在工作盆正上方, 并行 16 份配方.
- **蒸汽锯**: 动力锯, 并行 16 份配方, 加工速度是机械锯的 4 倍. 也可装在装置上.
- **高级注液器**: 5 tick 完成一次注液, 并行可在 1 到 20 之间用扳手打开侧面面板调整. 流体必须用管道输入, 不能手动倒.
- **青铜工作盆**: 可同时容纳多种流体, 因此需要多流体的搅拌与压缩配方成为可能, 每个流体槽的储量是普通工作盆的 16 倍.
- **青铜储罐(Bronze Depot)**: 沿用 Create 仓库行为的物品暂存方块, 与整套机器配套.

#### 兼容与扩展

- **KubeJS**: 可注册自定义锅炉, 太阳能锅炉, 燃烧室以及引擎与飞轮组合, 参数含 capacity, production, maxHeat, material, model. 燃料配方用 `solid_fuel(item, time).superheated()` 与 `fluid_fuel(fluid, time)` 编写.
- **JEI**: 提供固体燃料与流体燃料类别, 并在配方上标注加热与过热信息.
- **思索(Ponder)**: 内置场景覆盖锅炉机组, 太阳能锅炉, 蒸汽泵, 高级注液器与三台加工机器.
- **Create**: 所有机器接入常规应力网络, 蒸汽锯可在装置上工作.
- 以上每个数字都是 common 配置文件里的值: 热量档位, 效率阶梯, 三个过热倍率, 并行数与容量都可改.

#### 安装

Minecraft 1.20.1, Forge 47.x, Create 6.0 或更高版本. 把 jar 放进 `mods` 即可, 唯一必需的前置是 Create.

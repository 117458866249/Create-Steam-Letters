## 一句话简介 (Summary)

### English

A steam-driven Create processing line.

### 中文

使用蒸汽驱动的机械动力处理线路

## 详情 (Description)

### English

Inspired by Create: Steam Powered, we built a new Create processing line around steam, offering modpacks a richer set of gameplay options.

Steam production starts with the boiler. We reworked Create's own boiler so that a steam pump can draw steam or superheated steam out of it for use.

The compact boiler inspired by Steam Powered also produces steam in bulk, feeding the steam engine or the reworked boiler.

Engines and flywheels turn that steam into stress, driving Create machines while taking up less space.

Custom boilers, solar boilers, burners and engine/flywheel pairs can be registered with KubeJS.

The mod also adds bronze versions of the press, mixer, mechanical saw and basin. They consume steam to process recipes in parallel, speeding up Create's processing.

Note: this mod's tooltips and Ponder guides are not yet complete, stay tuned.

### 中文

受到Create:Steam Powered的启发, 我们基于`蒸汽`撰写了一套新的机械动力加工线路, 为整合包提供更加丰富的游玩体验

蒸汽的生产始于`锅炉`, 我们将机械动力原版的锅炉进行了修改, 可以使用`蒸汽泵`从锅炉中导出`蒸汽`或`过热蒸汽`以供使用

同时, 启发自`Steam Powered`的小型锅炉同样可以大量生产蒸汽, 供应`蒸汽引擎`或经过改动的`蒸汽锅炉`运行

使用`引擎与飞轮`将蒸汽转化为应力输出, 以更小的占地驱动机械动力设备运转

可使用`KubeJS`注册自定义锅炉, 太阳能锅炉, 燃烧室以及引擎与飞轮组合

本mod还添加了`辊压机`, `搅拌机`, `动力锯`与`工作盆`的青铜版本, 消耗蒸汽提供并行处理, 加快机械动力的处理速度

本mod的tooltip与思索引导尚未完成, 尽请期待

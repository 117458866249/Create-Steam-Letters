## 一句话简介 (Summary)

### English

A steam-driven Create processing line.

### 中文

使用蒸汽驱动的机械动力处理线路

## 详情 (Description)

### English

Inspired by Create: SteamPowered, we built a new Create processing line around steam, offering modpacks a richer set of gameplay options.

Steam production starts with the boiler. We reworked this mod's own boiler into a multi-block structure heated by blaze burners, producing steam or superheated steam that a steam pump can draw out for use.

Create's own boiler now runs on steam instead of producing it, feeding the steam engine or the reworked boiler.

Engines and flywheels turn that steam into stress, driving Create machines while taking up less space.

Custom boilers, solar boilers, burners and engine/flywheel pairs can be registered with KubeJS.

The mod also adds bronze versions of the press, mixer, mechanical saw and basin. They consume steam to process recipes in parallel, speeding up Create's processing.

Note: this Mod's Tooltips and Ponder guides are not yet complete, stay tuned.

### 中文

受到Create: SteamPowered的启发, 我们基于`蒸汽`撰写了一套新的机械动力加工线路, 为整合包提供更加丰富的游玩体验

蒸汽的生产始于锅炉, 我们将本模组的锅炉改造成可拼接的多方块结构, 以`烈焰人燃烧室`为热源生产`蒸汽`或`过热蒸汽`, 可通过`蒸汽泵`导出以供使用

同时, 机械动力原版的锅炉改为消耗`蒸汽`运行, 供应`蒸汽引擎`或经过改动的`蒸汽锅炉`运行

使用`引擎与飞轮`将蒸汽转化为应力输出, 以更小的占地驱动机械动力设备运转

可使用`KubeJS`注册自定义锅炉, 太阳能锅炉, 燃烧室以及引擎与飞轮组合

本Mod还添加了`辊压机`, `搅拌机`, `动力锯`与`工作盆`的青铜版本, 消耗蒸汽提供并行处理, 加快机械动力的处理速度
